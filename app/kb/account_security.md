# Account access and security

## Password resets
Customers can reset their password from the sign-in page using "Forgot password". Support can send a reset link only to the email address already on the account.

## Suspected account takeover
If a customer says someone else changed their password, email, or placed orders they did not make, treat it as a suspected account takeover. Never change the account email or share account details in the reply. Escalate to the security team immediately and tell the customer the account has been locked for their protection.

## Changing the account email
Support agents must never change the email address on an account through chat or email. The customer must do this themselves after signing in, or the security team does it after identity verification.
