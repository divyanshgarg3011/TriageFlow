# Shipping and delivery

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3–6 business days. Express shipping takes 1–2 business days. Orders ship within 24 hours on weekdays.

## Delayed or lost parcels
If tracking has not updated for 7 or more days, or a parcel has not arrived 10 days after it shipped, treat it as lost. Offer the customer a free reshipment of the same item or a full refund, whichever they prefer. Do not ask the customer to wait longer or to contact the carrier themselves.

## Wrong address
If the customer entered a wrong address and the order has not shipped, we can update it. Once shipped, we cannot redirect parcels.

## International orders
Customs duties are paid by the customer. Delays caused by customs are not treated as lost parcels until 21 days after shipping.
