# Returns and refunds policy

## Standard return window
Customers can return any unused item within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund. The item must be in its original packaging. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after we receive the item.

## Change of mind after 30 days
After 30 days from delivery we cannot offer a refund for change of mind. We can offer store credit worth 50% of the item price as a goodwill gesture, but only for Gold members.

## Defective or damaged items
If an item is defective or arrives damaged, the customer can choose a full refund or a free replacement within 90 days of delivery. No need to return packaging. Ask the customer for a short description or photo of the defect.

## Refund approval limits
Agents may approve refunds up to $500 per ticket. Any refund above $500 must be approved by a support manager before it is issued.

## Duplicate charges
If a customer was charged more than once for the same order, refund every extra charge immediately. Duplicate-charge refunds do not need the item to be returned.

## Return shipping
Return shipping is free for Gold members and for defective items. Standard members pay a flat $7.99 return shipping fee for change-of-mind returns.
