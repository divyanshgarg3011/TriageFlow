# Escalation rules

## Always escalate to a human
- Legal threats, mentions of lawyers, lawsuits, or chargebacks.
- Safety incidents: overheating, fire, injury, or property damage.
- Suspected account takeover or fraud.
- Refunds above $500.
- Any case where policy is unclear or the customer disputes a policy decision.

## Tone guidelines
Acknowledge the problem in the first sentence. Use the customer's first name. Be specific about what happens next and when. Never promise anything that policy does not allow, and never invent order details.

## Membership tiers
Gold members get free return shipping, priority handling, and goodwill store credit for late change-of-mind returns. Standard members get the standard policy.
