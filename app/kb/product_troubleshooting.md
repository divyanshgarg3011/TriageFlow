# Product troubleshooting

## Pulse smartwatch will not pair
Make sure Bluetooth is on and the Nimbus app is updated to the latest version. Remove the watch from the phone's Bluetooth list, then restart both devices. On the watch, go to Settings > System > Reset pairing, then pair again from the Nimbus app, not from the phone's Bluetooth menu. If it still fails, the customer can request a replacement under the defective-item policy.

## AeroBuds earbuds: one side silent
Place both earbuds in the case for 10 seconds, then hold the case button for 15 seconds until the light flashes white to reset them. Clean the charging contacts with a dry cloth.

## Volt power bank overheating
A power bank that becomes very hot, swells, smells of burning, or damages property is a safety incident. Tell the customer to stop using it immediately and keep it away from flammable items. Safety incidents must always be escalated to a human specialist; never troubleshoot them.

## Laptop screen flickering
Update the display driver from the Nimbus support site and check the issue in safe mode. If flickering appears in safe mode too, it is a hardware defect covered by the defective-item policy.
